# 🎨 Artist Co. — Creative Coloring Books for Kids

Official digital storefront and creative corner for **Artist Co. by Marvin Andrew**, publishers of *The Little Artists Bold & Easy Series*.

🌐 **Live Website:** [https://marvinandru.github.io/artist-co/](https://marvinandru.github.io/artist-co/)

---

## ✨ Features
- **7-Book Filterable Catalog:** Featuring adorable animals, sweet treats, dinosaurs, vehicles, and woodland friends.
- **Interactive Kids Digital Coloring Pad:** In-browser HTML5 canvas where kids can pick colors, paint live, and download their artwork.
- **5-Page Free Printable Sample Pack (PDF):** Downloadable high-resolution coloring pages for parents and teachers.
- **Pinterest Integration:** Vertical 2:3 share-ready graphics with instant 1-click Pin buttons.
- **Direct Amazon Prime Purchase Links:** Fast checkout for each printed book.
