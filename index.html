Meet the Creator

About Marvin Andrew & Artist Co.

Hi! I'm Marvin Andrew, founder of Artist Co. and publisher of the Little Artists Bold & Easy Series. Our mission is simple: to create wholesome, tactile, screen-free coloring books that children actually enjoy without getting frustrated by tiny, complex lines.

Partnered with our illustrator pen name Lily Sparkles, every single page is hand-crafted with extra-thick 3pt outlines, high-contrast black line art, and single-sided bleed protection so children can color with confidence.
